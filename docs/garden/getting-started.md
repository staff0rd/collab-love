# Getting started

Croydon Park, Sydney — temperate, frost-free. Seed data: [seeds.md](./seeds.md). Madeira vine: [madeira-vine.md](./madeira-vine.md).

**Two vines.** [Madeira vine](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Madeiravine) on one side — that ground is out, it comes up through anything you build. [Jasmine](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Jasmine) on the other, which won't. **Build on the jasmine side.**

## Track A — seedlings (start now)

**1. Sow the Jiffy strips.** One variety per strip. Daisy surface-sown at 1mm because it needs light; chrysanthemum at 4mm. A few seeds per pot, thinned to the strongest.

**2. Keep them warm.** Daisy 18–21°C, chrysanthemum 20–25°C — a sheltered warm spot, not the open garden. Peat pots dry fast, so check daily, and mist the daisy rather than watering it or you'll wash the seed off the surface.

**3. Grow on** to 4–6 true leaves.

## Track B — the bed (ready by October)

**1. Find the spot** on the jasmine side. 6+ hours of sun — check candidates at 9am, noon and 3pm on one clear day. Don't compromise on this.

**2. Prep it.** Cut the jasmine back off the fence and leave a gap you can reach into. Bag every clipping — jasmine layers, and a dropped fragment starts a new plant. Lay woven matting under the bed; permeable, so it won't trap water on the shale.

**3. Build it.** 40cm deep. Fork the subsoil first so it drains. Fill with garden mix and compost. Size follows the space.

**4. Get a soil thermometer.** The bed must read 18–21°C before anything goes in.

## Where they meet — plant out

October, at 4–6 true leaves. Plant the pot whole, **rim buried below the surface** or it wicks the rootball dry. Daisies south at 55cm spacing, chrysanthemums north at 35cm, so the 90cm ones don't shade the 60cm ones.

## Then, ongoing

Water every 2–3 days in summer heat, easing off for the daisies. Monthly liquid feed, lighter on the daisies. Deadhead weekly once flowering. Mulch before summer, clear of the stems. Pull weeds at the bed edges the week you see them. Watch whether the daisies need staking at 90cm.

## Later in the season

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Late Nov | Daisies flower |
| Jan | Chrysanthemums flower |
| Apr–May | Dry the daisy heads. Pull the chrysanthemum, save seed |
| Next Sep | Re-sow chrysanthemum. Daisies last a couple of years |
